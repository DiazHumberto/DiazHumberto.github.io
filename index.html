About me

I'm Humberto (Don) Díaz — an Environmental Engineer and GIS specialist based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. My work sits at the crossroads of spatial data, ecological science, and machine learning.

I hold a Bachelor of Environmental Engineering from Universidad Politécnica Salesiana and a Post-Degree Diploma in AI & Machine Learning from the University of Winnipeg (2026). I'm bilingual in English and Spanish and licensed to work across Canada.

From mapping restoration zones in the Galápagos Islands to building geospatial models for Canadian municipalities, I bring a rigorous, curious approach to every project.
