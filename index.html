Remote Sensing · Wetland Monitoring

Monitoring Wetland Vegetation Change in Manitoba Using Satellite Imagery and Python

Initial WMA analysis complete · Next phase in progress

Developed a Python-based remote sensing workflow to analyze vegetation change in and around Oak Hammock Marsh, Manitoba using Sentinel-2 imagery from August 2020 and August 2025. The workflow applies NDVI calculation, raster inspection, grid validation, change detection, classification, area statistics, and geospatial visualization.

The refined analysis focuses on the official Oak Hammock Marsh Wildlife Management Area and supports environmental monitoring by identifying areas of NDVI decrease, relative stability, and NDVI increase while clearly documenting limitations and next steps.

Python • Sentinel-2 • NDVI • Rasterio • GeoPandas • NumPy • Pandas • Matplotlib • Remote Sensing

Environmental AI · Wildfire Risk Analytics

Manitoba Wildfire Risk Intelligence

Active development · Data pipeline in progress

Developing a geospatial data science and machine-learning workflow to estimate daily wildfire ignition risk across Manitoba. The project combines historical wildfire records, a 10 km provincial analysis grid, ERA5-Land weather data, and spatial data processing to prepare a modelling-ready dataset for one-day-ahead wildfire risk prediction.

Current work includes cleaning Canadian wildfire records, validating fire locations against the Manitoba boundary, creating daily grid-cell fire targets, processing ERA5-Land weather variables, and combining weather features with wildfire occurrence targets for future predictive modelling.

Python • GeoPandas • Pandas • Xarray • ERA5-Land • Scikit-learn • GIS • Jupyter Notebook
